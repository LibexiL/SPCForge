# Changelog

## 1.2.0

National Hurricane Center added.

- **New Tropics tab:** every active storm NHC and CPHC are advising on (Atlantic, eastern and central Pacific), with its
  category, winds, pressure, movement and how far it is from you.
- **Storm pages:** the forecast cone, key messages, current wind field, wind-arrival times, wind-speed probabilities,
  peak storm surge and rainfall graphics (whichever NHC issues for that storm), the advisory summary, and the public advisory,
  forecast discussion, forecast advisory and wind-probability text.
- **Tropical weather outlooks** for each basin: the 7-day and 2-day graphics, the outlook text and the Tropical Weather Discussion.
- **Home** lists active storms and puts a banner at the top when one is within 600 miles of you.
- Choice chips in the middle of a page no longer scroll you back to the top.

## 1.1.0

Smoother motion throughout the app.

- **Tabs and screens slide and cross-fade:** opening a watch, MD or page slides in from the right, Back slides it away,
  and switching tabs eases toward the side of the tab you picked.
- **Bottom bar:** a highlight glides to the selected tab, its icon pops in and the colours fade across.
- **Content eases in** as it arrives from SPC (cards and list rows fade up with a slight stagger), and the page grows
  smoothly instead of jumping when maps and lists load.
- **Choice chips** (Day 1/2/3, Tornado/Wind/Hail, Today/Yesterday…) blend the highlight from the old choice to the new one,
  and scrolling chip rows keep their position.
- Maps fade in when they finish downloading; cards, rows and buttons press in slightly when tapped.
- Full-screen maps zoom open and closed, and double-tap zoom is animated.
- Storm-report totals count up; the refresh button spins; the title and back button fade between screens.
- Turning off animations in Android's settings (Developer options or Accessibility → Remove animations) turns these off too.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- **Home:** your outlook risk for today, Day 2 and Day 3 (with tornado / wind / hail probabilities), alerts when a watch or
  mesoscale discussion covers you, the Day 1 map, active watches and MDs, today's storm-report counts, Days 2–3 at a glance.
- **Outlooks:** Day 1, 2 and 3 categorical and probability maps, the risk-area tables, and the forecast discussion; Days 4–8.
- **Watches & mesoscale discussions:** every active one, with its graphic, text and (for watches) the probability table and PDS flag.
- **Storm reports:** today and yesterday, map plus a filterable list (tornado / wind / hail / significant); tap a report for a map.
- **More:** fire weather outlooks (Day 1, 2, 3–8), thunderstorm outlooks, SPC mesoanalysis (10 sectors, 30 parameters).
- Product text reformatted for phones, or shown exactly as issued; pinch-zoom on every graphic.
- Checks GitHub for new versions about once a day and installs them for you.
